# Editorial plan

## Current drafts

*Effective Agents* is the programmer's guide. Its [proposed table of contents](../books/effective-agents/README.md) and [first chapter](../books/effective-agents/01-describe-the-product.md) are ready for editorial review. The opening chapter is “Describe the product,” grounded in the source repositories and their early history. The previous generic task-direction part and fictional export chapter have been removed. The [source notes](../books/effective-agents/SOURCE_NOTES.md) record the complete retained component corpus, supporting examples and limits.

*Machina Practica* is a walkthrough of the agent skills: what each is intended to accomplish, why its instructions are written that way, and how to check and improve it. Its scope remains provisional and its manuscript is not yet drafted.

## Next work

1. Review the Effective Agents contents, teaching sequence and opening chapter.
2. Review each teaching claim against the linked source revisions and distinguish synthesis from observed history.
3. Draft the application-foundation chapter from the early shell commits and launch tests. Continue through MVP design and tracer planning, using actual project examples.
4. Outline Machina Practica around the goals, instructions, examples and checks for each skill.
5. Inspect concrete implementation and repair histories before drafting later chapters. Separately evaluate the extracted skills in authorized projects; do not treat the source projects as adopters.
6. Establish a Typst workflow for professional print editions from the Markdown manuscripts.

## Source and publication

Markdown is the canonical source for both books, readable by people and agents. See the [book source conventions](../books/README.md). Keep print layout separate from the prose. Typst conversion and print validation remain future work.

Eight experimental skills already exist. Their structural checks pass; their behavior in complete new projects remains to be evaluated. Book drafts and illustrative examples are not evidence that those evaluations have run.
