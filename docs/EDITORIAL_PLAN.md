# Editorial plan

## Current drafts

*Effective Agents* now opens with [Build a game: Deep Sea](../books/effective-agents/01-build-deep-sea.md), a hands-on exercise using short product requests and the framework skills. The [contents](../books/effective-agents/README.md) lead with that build and then explain its stages. [Editorial source notes](../books/effective-agents/SOURCE_NOTES.md) hold the original research and link the independent build trial used to check the lesson.

*Machina Practica* is a walkthrough of the agent skills: what each is intended to accomplish, why its instructions are written that way, and how to check and improve it. Its scope remains provisional and its manuscript is not yet drafted.

## Next work

1. Review the opening exercise for practical usability: prompts, prerequisites, expected results and manual checks.
2. Build on the completed [Deep Sea trial](trials/deepsea/README.md) with additional readers, hosts and target environments.
3. Fix recurring procedure in skills or components and rerun the affected work; keep those instructions out of reader prompts.
4. Draft later chapters as practical explanations of the stages, keeping research comparisons in source notes.
5. Outline Machina Practica around the goals, instructions, examples and checks for each skill.
6. Establish a Typst workflow for professional print editions from the Markdown manuscripts.

## Source and publication

Markdown is the canonical source for both books, readable by people and agents. See the [book source conventions](../books/README.md). Keep print layout separate from the prose. Typst conversion and print validation remain future work.

Eight experimental skills already exist. Their structural checks pass, and the Deep Sea trial adds direct behavioral evidence. Book prose is not evidence on its own; retain the requests, outputs, observed failures and validation limits.
