# Two connected books

Both books start from the same premise: precise intent, clear operating constraints, and strong feedback loops make agent-assisted work more reliable.

<h2 id="effective-agents">Effective Agents</h2>

For programmers, tech leads, and teams directing coding agents. The book will explain task framing, supervision, review, and integration while preserving human engineering responsibility.

<h2 id="machina-practica">Machina Practica</h2>

For agents and the humans configuring their repositories. The book will explain the rules, skills, and verification practices that make the method repeatable.

**Status: manuscripts are not yet available.** The [vision](/vision/) is the current source of editorial intent. [Practica on GitHub](https://github.com/machinapractica/practica) owns both books and their future supporting assets.
