# Business and protocol source checklist

Identify participants, authority, terminology, inputs, outputs, lifecycle transitions, time semantics, exceptional termination, corrections, and externally required invariants. Distinguish observed practice, contractual requirement, and proposed process change.

For uptime: distinguish an observation from an incident, acknowledgement from resolution, and maintenance suppression from success. For a protocol: include version, framing, ordering, retries/idempotency, invalid inputs, and compatibility. Describe these as external behavior, not the proposed application architecture.

Cite supplied records or authoritative specifications. Sanitized examples should carry their provenance; do not copy customer data or credentials into fixtures.
